# TerraBird

I'm not an experienced coder, not even a coder, yet. This will be the first project I've coded. So I'm hoping to start simple. I hope you don't mind. 
The plan is a Flappy bird variation, but with the jumping function replaced with switching the direction of gravity. 
Basic, I know.

Functions: 
- coins
    - you can gather coins by going through pillars, getting achievements, or picking them up in Mario World. 
    - you can speed up your coin gathering process by going into the backrooms
    - you can spend your coins in the **store**
- store
    - in the store you can open your achievements
    - but you can also buy different trails and parachutes. there may be a third buyable item, like a background or special columns. 
- multiple dimensions
    - you can enter one of 2 (soon to be 3) dimensions by crashing into specific pillars. You'll know when you see them. 
    - you stand to win skins and coins, don't lose this chance. 
- Backgrounds depending on the time of day.

Note on AI use: I give this a 9/10 on AI use. I didn't just ask claude to build it, but i doubt there was a single paragraph I didn't as help for from AI. 

Controls: 
P or esc to pause
space, click, or arrow buttons (up and down) to flip gravity
